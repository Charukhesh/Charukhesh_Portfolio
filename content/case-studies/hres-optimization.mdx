## Overview

Sizing a hybrid renewable-plus-storage plant is usually optimized against a levelized-cost objective that ignores
contract structure. This project embeds the actual commercial terms,  IRR targets, PPA shortfall penalties, tax
shields, and asset degradation, directly into the sizing optimization, and stress-tests reliability with a
long-horizon Monte Carlo simulation rather than a single design-year estimate.

## Problem formulation

The sizing problem is posed as a multi-objective optimization over capacity decisions $x$ (solar, wind, storage):

$$
\max_{x} \; \text{IRR}(x) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbb{E}[\text{PPA shortfall penalty}(x)] \le \epsilon, \;\; \text{Reliability}(x) \ge \Delta R
$$

Renewable generation is not sampled from historical traces directly, but from a Delta-Distribution Mixture of
Gaussians fit to produce noise-free synthetic profiles, with candidate mixtures compared via KL-divergence to
check statistical convergence to the empirical distribution.

## Results

Manuscript titled *"Contractual and resource-aware capacity optimization for hybrid renewable energy systems
(HRES) with storage"* is currently under review at *Applied Energy* (Elsevier); the associated work was accepted
for presentation at the SGAI Conference, Lisbon, Portugal, 2026.

## Industrial application

| | |
|---|---|
| **Industry** | Energy, independent power producers, renewable project finance |
| **Problem** | Sizing hybrid renewable + storage assets against real contractual and financial exposure, not just levelized cost |
| **How it helps** | Directly optimizing IRR net of PPA penalties and tax shields under long-horizon Monte Carlo reliability constraints gives asset owners a sizing decision that reflects actual downside risk, not an idealized average year |
| **Deployment path** | Industry collaboration (Hero Future Energies) → Monte Carlo validation → operational sizing tool |
| **Who cares** | Energy asset developers, project-finance teams, renewable IPPs sizing hybrid plants |
