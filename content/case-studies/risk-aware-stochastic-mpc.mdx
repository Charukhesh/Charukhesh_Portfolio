## Overview

This project implements a Python based UAV motion planning and control system that replicates and extends the hierarchical planning framework of Bui et al. (2024). The system tightly integrates local perception, occupancy grid mapping, A* global reference planning, and nonlinear Model Predictive Control (MPC) while introducing probabilistic collision risk constraints to handle perception uncertainty dynamically.

## Technical approach

Obstacle detections are modeled with Gaussian uncertainty. Instead of relying on fixed physical margins, a probabilistic chance constraint limits collision risk to approximately 1%. The required clearance margin continuously scales based on the obstacle's spatial uncertainty and the UAV's view geometry. The nonlinear MPC tracks the A* reference path while respecting strict velocity and acceleration constraints, replanning in a receding horizon fashion.

## Results

Achieved 12% lower completion time and 20% lower control effort compared to a conservatively tuned deterministic baseline in simulated corridor navigation with anisotropic sensor noise.
