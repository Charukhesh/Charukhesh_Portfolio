## Overview

Physical assets exposed to non-stationary risk (e.g. energy assets under shifting weather/market regimes) need
controllers that hedge tail outcomes, not just expected cost. This ongoing thesis develops a real-time control loop
that couples a CVaR-constrained MPC formulation with sequential Bayesian state estimation, so the risk model itself
adapts as the underlying regime shifts.

## Problem formulation

$$
\min_{u_{0:T}} \; \mathbb{E}\Big[\sum_{k=0}^{T} c(x_k,u_k)\Big] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{CVaR}_\alpha\big(L(x_{0:T})\big) \le \gamma
$$

where the latent risk state is tracked online via sequential Bayesian updates as new observations arrive, rather
than assumed fixed at design time.

## Approach so far

Adaptive state-space models with sequential Bayesian inference are used to estimate latent risk states and update
forecasts, feeding a CVaR-constrained MPC layer that re-solves the control problem as new information arrives.

## Limitations

> This is ongoing thesis work — results, benchmarks and final control formulation are not yet finalized and are
> intentionally not quantified here.
